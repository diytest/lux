Luxmeters, colorimeter

Pocket graphical luxmeter LG05 USB.


Luxmeter LG05 USB - standalone pocket meter with a USB interface,
capable of recording the signal received by its photodiode, converting it into an array
and displaying the graph and signal parameters - minimum, maximum, average value, ripple factor, and frequency.
40 arrays can be recorded into the non-volatile memory of LG05.
Connected via USB, the luxmeter can transfer recorded arrays to a computer for their display on the computer and archiving.


Graphical luxmeter LG09 USB.

Luxmeter LG09 USB:  electronic module with a head with frosted glass, nozzle nearby
LG09 is designed for measuring brightness, illumination, and thermal radiation (radiometer mode).
LG09  is controlled and powered via USB by a personal computer or laptop (in the future - by a pocket computer or smartphone).
Like most luxmeters, LG09 is capable of measuring the parameters of continuous and periodic light signals.


Along with using various light-sensitive heads and nozzles, recording signals and displaying them in graphical form, LG09 allows measuring temperature.

Comparison colorimeter KS08 USB
4 color channels, with 8 measuring ranges in each, USB interface, software

The colorimeter is designed for objective evaluation of color and chromaticity.

Colorimeter KS08 determines color and chromaticity coordinates of non-self-luminous and self-luminous objects;
connects to a computer's USB and works with a computer application,
The application allows users to expand the library of standards, samples, loci, and frosted glasses.
