Comparison colorimeter KS08 USB

KS08, 4 color channels, with 8 measuring ranges in each, USB interface, software

Article about a light-measuring test bench based on a colorimeter , Magazine "METROLOGIYA TA PRYLADY" • №6’2010

Colorimeter KS08 determines color and chromaticity coordinates of non-self-luminous and self-luminous objects;
connects to a computer's USB and works with a computer application,
The application allows users to expand the library of standards, samples, loci, and frosted glasses.

The colorimeter is designed for objective evaluation of color and chromaticity.

Why does a colorimeter have 4 color channels, 

instead of 3 (RGB)? A TV set, which operates with 3 colors, does not accurately reproduce, for example, aqua color (blue-green) or magenta.
4-channel colorimeters can determine a wider range of colors.

The colorimeter has 4 color channels, each channel is equipped with an 8-range signal meter (ordinary luxmeters have 3 ranges).

The colorimeter will be useful in industries where it is necessary to reproduce or compare the color, brightness, contrast, and saturation of objects. For example, when dyeing leather, fabrics, in the manufacture of varnishes, paints, perfumes (nail polish, lipstick), in photo labs and printing houses, in the food industry (juices, confectionery), in the manufacture of colored solutions (chemistry, medicine), plastics, etc.

The colorimeter is made as a single device connected to the computer's USB, power - via USB from the computer.
Dimensions: diameter 15mm, length 50mm. 

Photocurrents from the channels of the colorimeter enter the channel amplifiers with automatically switchable measuring ranges.
Each of the 4 converter-amplifiers can operate in one of the 8 ranges, and the ranges are switched for each of the channels individually.
The gain factors corresponding to the ranges are 800, 400, 200, 80, 40, 20, 4 and 1.

The colorimeter is capable of determining the period for periodic signals (lighting with lamps powered by alternating current, computer monitors, etc.), which allows calculating the average value of periodic signals over an integer number of periods.
This makes it possible to significantly reduce the measurement error performed by the colorimeter, compared to the error obtained when calculating the average over a random (non-integer) number of periods.

A library is supplied with the software which the user can update independently (add parameters of new standards, areas, frosted glasses, samples, loci, by entering their coordinates).

The main part of the colorimeter is the measuring head,
four light filters are visible on the left, the controller board is fixed on the right, the meter diameter is 70mm, the height without the cover is 50mm.

Photocurrents from the 4 photodiodes of the meter, each of which is equipped with its own light filter, enter 4 amplifiers with automatically switchable measuring ranges. 
Each amplifier can operate in one of the 5 ranges, and the ranges are switched for each of the channels individually. The maximum measurable values of photocurrents by ranges are:  10nA, 100nA, 1µA, 10µA, 100µA. The resolution for the 10nA range is 10pA.

The measuring head transmits data through the COM port to the computer, is powered by a power adapter, and a variant with operation and power via USB is possible.  

For periodic signals (lighting with lamps powered by alternating current, computer monitors, etc.), the period is determined, which allows calculating the average value of periodic signals over an integer number of periods.
This makes it possible to significantly reduce the measurement error
compared to the error obtained when calculating the average over a random (non-integer) number of periods.

The computer application provides about 20 measurement and information display modes.

In the colorimeter , metrological photodiodes with a large sensitive element area are used, respectively, with high sensitivity.
